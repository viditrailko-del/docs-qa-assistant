Answers you can check, or an honest "the documents don't say".

Ask a question about a company's policies. The assistant finds the most relevant passages, and Gemini answers only from them, quoting the exact words behind every sentence. Then plain code with no AI checks each quote against the passage it cites and each number against the cited text. What can't be verified is shown as unverified; what the documents don't cover is refused.

Question→ Search 1,407 passages→ Gemini answers with quotes→ Checks in code→ Verified, flagged or refused
The documents are a fixed snapshot of 60 pages of the public GitLab Handbook (expenses, travel, benefits, leave, hiring, communication). This demo is not affiliated with GitLab. Please don't type personal data: questions are stored until the nightly reset.
